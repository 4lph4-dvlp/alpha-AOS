## Delivery/cancellation race and review binding

All mutating bootstrap operations consume the previewed planDigest and expected record/content revision from their schema variant, rechecked inside the same held writer session. A claim grants one bounded delivery intent, not an irrevocable external-process transaction. The host must inspect/recheck the claim/cancellation immediately before native invocation and record/link the exact native reference at the first allocation boundary. Cancellation before any claim proves zero pending starts; cancellation racing an already returned claim must report delivery-in-flight/needs-reconcile and route actual started work to native stop, rather than falsely claiming no process could start. It issues no further dispatch permission and never auto-clears ambiguous intent. Test both serialized outcomes and the return-to-host gap. The product guarantees conservative duplicate prevention, not exactly-once transport or atomicity across a chat/native workflow boundary.
