<threat_model>
| STRIDE Category | Threat Description | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Denial of Service | Unbounded child process hangs indefinitely or consumes infinite CPU | Mandatory 15-minute timeout with whole-tree process termination (D-13). |
| Denial of Service | Child process spams infinite stdout/stderr to exhaust system memory | Hard stream buffer cap of 10MB triggering process tree termination (D-13). |
| Repudiation | Cancelled or killed processes lose forensic output needed to diagnose issues | Retain, redact, excerpt (up to 64KB), and sha256-hash all stream bytes captured before kill signal (D-16). |
| Tampering | Orphan grandchild processes persist in background after parent is killed | Cross-platform tree termination targeting PID tree (/T on Windows, -pid group on POSIX) (D-16). |
</threat_model>
