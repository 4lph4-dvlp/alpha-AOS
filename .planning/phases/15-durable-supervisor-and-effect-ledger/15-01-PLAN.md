| Tampering | Partial or torn writes to checkpoint.json during sudden power loss or process kill | Write to temporary file with handle.sync(), atomic rename, and syncDirectory (D-01, D-02). |
| Denial of Service | Unbounded child process output fills disk or exhausts memory | Enforce 64KB per-stream output excerpt budget with sha256 summary (D-03). |
