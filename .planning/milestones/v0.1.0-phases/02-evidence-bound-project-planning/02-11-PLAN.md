`planSubProject`/`describeSubProjects` recurse with a member path the ladder undoes the descent, the

the real `src` is then excluded as already-visited — so every `kind: directory` fact answers
negatively and the emitted reason states that `src` does not exist while it demonstrably does.

matches and non-matches". CR-01 makes the first half unreachable on the most common real shape;
CR-02 makes the second half emit a statement that is false rather than merely incomplete.
